# Show components in turns

Choose **Edit layout**, select a component, then choose **Show in turns** in
Content. That component becomes the first item in an area with the same size
and position. Other display components keep their space.

Choose **Add item** to move another component from the display, use a saved
design, or create a custom item. Moving frees its former position in the draft.
Saved designs become independent copies. Items keep their text sizes and
bindings. A larger design must be edited to fit, or you can explicitly resize
the shared area when there is enough free space. Neighbours are never moved
automatically. Each area can hold up to eight items.

Use **Previous**, **Next** or an item's **Preview** button to inspect each view.
These controls only change your browser preview, including after you save.
Use **Edit design** to open the familiar component designer, then **Apply to
item** to return to the layout draft. Library Save design remains separate.
Move items up/down to set order, or Remove to remove a copy from the area.
The final item is kept; **Keep as single component** removes rotation when only
one item remains. Undo or Discard can recover draft changes.

Open **Timing & pause** to change the five-minute default or pause rotation.
When paused, choose which item to keep showing. Use **Save to display** to apply
your item, order and timing edits. The **On display** line reports acknowledged
content independently of the item you are previewing.

Time per item starts when it has been successfully shown. Screen spacing,
quiet hours, recovery and full cleanup can delay a switch. Changing data within
an item does not restart its time. After quiet hours the next item gets a full
turn; missed turns are not replayed. A restart begins with the first item (or
the configured paused item). Keep urgent information in a pinned component.

In Position, size the shared area to fit its largest item. Smaller items keep
their own dimensions at the top-left, leaving blank space inside the reserved
rectangle. Style's Update method combines with each member's preference (editable in that
item's designer under Style);
screen-wide cleanup limits always apply. Data fetching continues on its own
schedule and is not triggered by rotation.
