The data is provided as is. The NTA is not responsible for errors or inaccuracies.
Paperphase filters, sorts, limits and reformats the source for display; this is
not an official NTA or Dublin Bus product and no endorsement is implied.

Reference tables are derived from the NTA realtime-operator schedule archive.
They retain selected stop, route, trip and feed-version columns, plus timetable
rows for configured stops and service calendars/exceptions; shapes are omitted. Real-time data comes from the subscribed NTA
GTFS-Realtime TripUpdates feed. Keep attribution and this notice with shared
plugin packages and retain the template's compact attribution on displays.

Committed protobuf/ZIP/JSON fixtures are synthetic examples of the public
formats, not captures containing a user's key. The wire reader follows the
public GTFS-Realtime specification:
https://gtfs.org/documentation/realtime/reference/

The data licence is separate from Paperphase's code licence.
